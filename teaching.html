Working with students

At the University of Luxembourg I work closely with four doctoral and two master's students on their research, with a role that ranges from proposing the research question to helping them structure work they had already started. Recent work with students includes detecting malicious agent skills across public skill marketplaces, which started as a master's project, and benchmarking face recognition with synthetic data. During my Ph.D., I co-supervised a master's thesis at Politecnico di Milano.
