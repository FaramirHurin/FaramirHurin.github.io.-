Daniele Lunghi

Postdoctoral researcher at SnT, University of Luxembourg.
Machine learning security: large language models and agents, fraud detection, federated learning, face recognition.

I work on the security of large language models and agents: red-teaming and jailbreaking, prompt injection in agent skill marketplaces, and inference-time methods that steer models toward safer, higher-quality output. I did my Ph.D. at the Université libre de Bruxelles and the Athena Research Center as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellow, working on adversarial attacks against credit card fraud detection, and during it I spent three months as a visiting researcher at Worldline in Lyon. Before that, I was a machine learning intern at the European Central Bank.
